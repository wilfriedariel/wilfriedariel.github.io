About Me
  • I am a PhD student at NTU CCDS × Nanyang Business School, GIFTS Lab. My advisors are Prof. Will Cong (previously at Cornell) and Prof. Gao Cong. I work on questions at the boundary of quantitative finance, decentralized finance, and AI.
  • Before starting my PhD, I was an AI Research Engineer at Singapore Management University, working on vision-language models, cultural AI, and ASEAN benchmarks with Prof. Chong-Wah Ngo. Before that, a Research Intern at NTUST CITI Lab in Taipei under Prof. Shuo Yan Chou, working on RFMS and outcome economy systems.
  • I did my undergraduate at Institut Teknologi Bandung. My thesis was on building ML-based trading strategies for Indonesia's LQ45 index, covering signal design, feature engineering, and systematic backtesting.
  • Outside of research, I managed an undisclosed prediction market fund as Managing Director, with 7-figure USD in volume. Real-world market forecasting is something I think about a lot.
Research Interests

I build AI systems that sit at the junction of financial markets, economic mechanisms, and social systems. Deployability and interpretability matter as much to me as the math.

Finance & Economic AI
I study how AI can be applied to quantitative finance, DeFi protocols, and economic modeling. This covers algorithmic trading and market microstructure, token mechanism design, and decentralized governance.
FinTech AI Quantitative Finance AI for Economics Decentralized Finance
Society, Ethics & Education
I am interested in how AI shapes and is shaped by social systems. This includes questions of fairness, accountability, and how to design AI that opens up access to education and knowledge rather than closing it off.
AI Ethics AI for Education Social Computing
Methods & Theory
On the methods side, I work on real-world model evaluation, explainable AI, and game-theoretic approaches to multi-agent systems. I am particularly drawn to settings where incentive alignment and strategic behavior matter.
Real-World Models Explainable AI AI in Game Theory
