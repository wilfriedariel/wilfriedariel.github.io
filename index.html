Wilfried Ariel Mulyawan 陳順福

PhD Student · NTU CCDS × Nanyang Business School · GIFTS Lab

Wilfried Ariel Mulyawan is a PhD Student at Nanyang Technological University, jointly between the College of Computing & Data Science and Nanyang Business School, in the GIFTS Lab. He is advised by Prof. Will Cong (previously Cornell) and Prof. Gao Cong. His research sits at the intersection of quantitative finance, decentralized finance, and AI — with a focus on how machine learning systems can be designed, evaluated, and deployed in real financial and economic environments.

Before NTU, Wilfried was an AI Research Engineer at Singapore Management University with Prof. Chong-Wah Ngo, working on vision–language models, cultural AI, and ASEAN benchmarks (EMNLP 2025). He completed his B.Eng. at Institut Teknologi Bandung, with a thesis on machine-learning trading strategies for Indonesia's LQ45 index. Outside research, he served as Managing Director of an undisclosed prediction market fund.
